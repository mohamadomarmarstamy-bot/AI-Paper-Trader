# Jarvis paper challenger rotation and learning

Current Jarvis and research portfolios create a learning checkpoint after each 100 distinct fully closed coins. Coins shared across strategies count once; coins still held are deferred. Reports separate strategy versions, include recorded fees and write-offs, and describe evidence rather than award promotion. Each checkpoint makes the next cloud strategy review due, subject to the existing reservation budget, incident priority and queue gates. Daily review remains a fallback.

The older preset learner now reviews every 100 new completed positions after its unchanged initial 1,000-position warmup. This legacy counter is separate from current Jarvis learning. Per-strategy promotion still requires 100 distinct coins and its existing profitability and robustness checks.

A queued cloud candidate must pass the existing data-feasibility scouting gate before it can displace a trial. If all slots are occupied, Jarvis may pause one empty testing slot idle for at least two hours. It preserves the original rule, history, cash and funding. Held positions and proven strategies are protected. Owner pauses and exit backlog protection take precedence. There is at most one active or draining rotation and at most two starts per rolling 24 hours.

The challenger gets six hours to open paper positions. At the deadline entries stop and existing positions finish under their normal exits. If the challenger earns proven status under unchanged validation rules, it stays. Otherwise, once the slot is empty, the original strategy resumes with a new data-discovery cutoff and its existing evidence. A window ending is not a profitability failure. Unexpected slot ownership is flagged for review instead of overwritten.

Paused, resumed and learning events appear in the existing summary email. No laptop update, new credentials, balance reset, real-money permission or budget increase is required.
