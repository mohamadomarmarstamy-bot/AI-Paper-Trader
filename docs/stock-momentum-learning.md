# Broad stock PAPER momentum filters and 25-trade adjustments

The stock Settings page controls price range (initial $2–$20), reported float range (initial 0–20 million shares), cumulative daily volume divided by the mean of the 20 preceding daily sessions (initial 2x), average daily volume (250,000 shares), current bid/ask spread (0.5%), day change (2%), optional opening gap, additional ATR cap (8%), and optional timestamped news within 24 hours. A zero threshold disables that individual additional check. Enabled checks reject missing evidence. The legacy scanner score/freshness, 8% ATR ceiling and all broker risk checks still apply. News is off by default: a recent headline is not confirmation of an earnings/FDA/contract catalyst.

These controls act in the final broad automatic BUY adapter, after its current risk quote and before order submission. They do not govern manual Pro Ticker submissions or meme trading. Price is the current ask. Float uses Yahoo Finance floatShares, never sharesOutstanding; filing date is unavailable and values are cached up to 24 hours. Two in-flight fundamental requests, a 1.5-second decision wait and failure cooldown bound application work. Daily volume ratio is not time-of-day adjusted RVOL, and data availability limits coverage. Orders can fill beyond observed prices. These filters do not establish profitability.

A snapshot keyed by accepted broker entry order ID preserves pre-entry features and settings revision, including delayed fills. An evidence-write failure after acceptance is logged and does not resubmit the order. The worker reviews every 30 seconds and consumes exactly 25 new unique CLOSED learning outcomes linked to auto_trader_entry trade-book rows. Open positions, partial closes, manual Pro Ticker and meme trades do not count. Installation anchors at the existing learning-outcome ID; old historical outcomes are not replayed. Pausing adjustment skips outcomes collected while paused.

Each batch reports winner/loser feature medians, missing counts, P/L and its exact outcome IDs. Filter fitting uses 17 training rows and 8 later validation rows, comparable stop/target policies, at least three wins/losses with the relevant feature, training retention of 10, and validation retention of four. It can make one 10% tightening when the retained validation mean is positive and improves by more than 0.05 percentage points. This small observational validation is an experiment, not proof of a durable edge; multiple tested filters can overfit.

If a complete 25-trade batch loses money without a validated filter candidate, the learner reduces future position size by 10%, down to 0.1% of equity. It never increases exposure, widens stops, or raises loss limits to force a change. Positive batches without an improvement and losing batches already at the floor retain settings with an explicit reason.

Settings, actor stock_auto_25 audit, immutable batch report and consumed cursor commit atomically under the same database write lock. Failed writes roll back together. Concurrent manual settings are read after acquiring the lock; automatic risk reductions use those latest values. Restart/repeated reviews do not consume a batch twice. Cache invalidation happens after commit. Existing native protective orders retain their protections. Settings shows progress, before/after changes, and reasons; normal Undo remains available subject to revision checks. Recorded PAPER returns do not fully model execution costs or fees.

## Follow-up experiments, intraday timing and estimated costs (October 29 update)

- Intraday mode defaults on: at least 1.5× same-time cumulative volume, maximum 5% above session VWAP, and a completed five-minute breakout or VWAP pullback. Its volume threshold replaces the daily ratio gate while enabled. Manual Pro Ticker and meme trades retain separate controls.
- Read-only Alpaca IEX 5-minute bars cover 35 calendar days, with bounded pagination (3×10,000) and a 60-second cache. Current/incomplete candles, premarket, after-hours, future sessions and malformed/duplicate bars cannot establish evidence. Historical prices/volumes use split adjustment. The comparison uses up to 20 preceding sessions with at least 80% interval coverage at an identical ET cutoff, minimum five sessions. Four completed current bars and contiguous recent setup bars are required; missing required evidence rejects the buy. IEX is one venue, not consolidated market volume. No time-of-day seasonal curve or prices are invented.
- A breakout closes above the previous three completed bars' highs with a positive candle. A pullback has a previous low within 1% above session VWAP or lower, followed by a positive candle closing above VWAP and the previous close. VWAP is volume-weighted from provider bar VWAPs. Current risk-checked ask is used for distance above VWAP. Broker risk/quote admission refreshes after the potentially slow data lookup. The existing legacy score/freshness/ATR restrictions still apply.
- `GET /auto-trader/stock-entry-timing?symbol=...` is authenticated and read-only. It reports available evidence or an explicit missing/outside-session reason, and never submits orders.
- Cost-adjusted batch reports subtract `2 × (slippage_bps_per_side + fee_bps_per_side)` from recorded return in basis points. Defaults: 10 bps slippage per side, 0 extra fees. Monetary costs use entry price × learning-outcome shares. These are configurable **incremental stress costs** beyond paper broker fills; broker-recorded P/L stays unchanged. The learner now uses estimated net returns/P/L, including for the losing-batch size reduction. All trades in a review use the reported cost model consistently; taxes are excluded.
- A filter change opens one persistent experiment. Only the first 25 fully closed automatic trades whose accepted entries have the exact new settings revision and a capture timestamp after the change count toward follow-up. Pre-change positions closing later are excluded. No further parameter change occurs while evaluating this experiment. Defensive size reductions never open a reversible experiment.
- Follow-up estimated mean return more than 0.1 percentage point worse than the baseline triggers rollback of the changed filter if rollback is enabled. It is an observational check, not causal proof. The audit, experiment decision and batch cursor commit atomically; restarts cannot repeat them. Any newer saved settings revision supersedes a pending experiment so manual edits are preserved. No automatic rollback raises position size or risk limits. Successful/no-worse experiments are marked kept; comparisons and actual before/after audit values appear in Settings.
- Regular-cycle broker marks are sampled for open broad automatic stock trades. Observed best/worst returns and giveback are persisted by trade-book ID. Research-only shadow exits compare 1% stop/2% target, 1.5%/3%, and 2%/4%, using the first observed mark crossing a threshold (not an assumed fill at that threshold); absent crossings use the actual closing return. Only trades with observations enter the comparison, with explicit sample coverage. Partial paths, gaps between samples, execution latency and the counterfactual market path limit interpretation. This report **does not change existing or future stop/target orders**.
- Controls and reports are available in the existing Settings screen on desktop/mobile. The automatic worker remains PAPER-only and has no broker-order access.
