# Solana copy PAPER experiment

## Current profit protection — October 30, 2026

Staged half-sales default on (`profit_take_enabled`), starting at a 20% net gain (`profit_take_first_percent`, configurable above zero through 100%). Sell half of the remaining token quantity, retain half, and double the next gain threshold: 20%, 40%, 80%, 160%, and onward. Each stage uses the currently observed modeled net exit value against the remaining allocated entry cost, not a historical high or the source trader's fill. A jump over several levels makes one half-sale at the observed quote and advances above that observed gain; repeated marks cannot drain the lot at the same price. Stages and the next threshold persist across cycles/restarts. The first-threshold setting applies to untrimmed lots; changing it does not reset an existing ladder. Skipped entry signals are never replayed.

Every half-sale allocates half the remaining quantity and entry cost, charges 1% plus $0.10 on that actual modeled sale, credits cash, and archives only that slice's realized P/L. The original lot identity and remaining entry pool/quantity/cost remain in the open holding; the coin exposure slot is occupied until the remainder fully closes. Sale proceeds must exceed the slice's allocated cost, so the fixed charge cannot turn a supposed profit-taking sale into a realized loss. Realized P/L and cost-based returns aggregate sold slices only, without double-counting entry charges or valuing unsold quantity as realized. The UI labels this as Realized exits, separately counts partial sales and fully closed lots, and shows remaining quantity, banked P/L and the next target.

After the first half-sale, a net break-even trigger closes the remainder when its observed modeled exit value falls to its remaining allocated entry cost. This aims to retain banked gains while leaving the runner uncapped; it is not a guarantee against gaps, missing quotes, costs or provider delay. `profit_protection` full exits share the coin-wide cooldown with `stop_loss` exits; partial profit sales and source/manual exits do not start the cooldown. Disabling new profit trims leaves previously armed break-even protection in force. Manual/source sells take priority over new profit trims, selling only the remainder with one exit charge; a source sell does not reconstruct any previously sold quantity. Existing hard stops and runner break-even protection execute before potentially slow source polling. Profit trims run after source signals are processed, including when entries are paused or wallet polling is unavailable, using a current eligible exact-mint quote. Missing quotes never invent a sale. Existing holdings adopt the rule on their next actual observation; prior highs are never backfilled.

All changes are isolated to the PAPER copy account. The normal crypto scanner is unchanged. The disabled Real Copy Trade blueprint inherits the canonical settings/rules; no real execution, signing, spending authority or wallet mutation is enabled.

## Public RPC backlog recovery — October 30, 2026

Signature pages now explicitly request up to 1,000 entries instead of 20, within Solana's documented maximum and the same request-rate/body-size limits. Large streams of failed transactions are counted and excluded before transaction decoding. Full pages still require pagination to the existing cursor/time boundary; neither the follow cursor nor historical holdings are reset. Failed-transaction counts for a window started before this update are labeled incomplete (at least the displayed count).

Signature-list requests have an eight-second timeout; individual transaction requests retain five seconds. If a decode request fails or a recent confirmed transaction is not yet available, the fetched history page and successfully decoded prefix are retained, with only the unresolved suffix retried. The original cursor remains in force until that frozen window completes, and pending signals are not released early. Error details and successful coverage timestamps appear in the copy workspace. Other wallets retain the existing oldest-poll-first scheduling and error isolation; existing lots are marked/protected before wallet polling. No immediate retries bypass RPC cooldowns, failed transactions never become copy signals, stale buys remain unfilled, and provider availability is not guaranteed.

## Current policy — October 30, 2026: coin exposure and stop-loss cooldown

After the Spacecat repeated-add/stop-loss sequence, the owner requested protection against accumulating full-size lots and immediately buying back into the same drop. `max_open_lots_per_coin` now defaults to one, shared across all followed wallets and pools for the exact mint. At the current $50 trade size this permits one $50 lot plus its $0.10 entry charge, not multiple $50 add-ons. Pending sells and unpriced holdings still occupy their slots. The configurable limit must be a positive whole number. Reducing it does not liquidate existing lots. A source add-on can still qualify if no copied lot occupies the slot and the coin is not cooling down; source pre-holdings alone do not reject it.

`stop_loss_cooldown_seconds` defaults to 1,800 seconds. Only an actual modeled `stop_loss` close starts or extends the mint-wide deadline, across every followed wallet. Source/manual exits do not start it. Deadlines persist across reloads and restarts; on upgrade they are seeded from recent archived stop closes, without rewriting history or closing existing positions. Changes to duration apply to future stops (zero disables future stop cooldowns). The old `reentry_cooldown_seconds` and `mint_cooldowns` remain compatibility data and do not gate entries. Public entry protection reports the effective stop-loss cooldown and its trigger.

Each buy is checked against the currently held lots and stop deadlines sequentially, including same-cycle bursts and stops before wallet polling. Buys observed before cooldown expiry or originating during the cooldown are skipped permanently; only a new source buy after expiry can qualify. Recently expired deadlines are retained through the 90-second signal window to reject delayed pre-expiry buys, then pruned. Signal identity deduplication remains; repeated polling and freeing a slot never replay skipped buys. Stale (>90-second), pre-enrollment, already-sold, unsupported, paused, unpriced and unfunded buys remain unfilled. No liquidity/volume entry gate is introduced. Clear cap/cooldown reasons and expiry times appear in Latest signals, with both controls exposed in settings.

`stop_loss_percent` defaults to 20 and accepts 0–100 (0 disables it). Every lot, including pre-existing holdings, closes when current modeled net exit proceeds are at or below `cost_usd * (1 - stop_loss_percent / 100)`. A $50 buy has $50.10 total modeled cost, so the 20% loss target is about $10.02. Marks already include 1% modeled exit slippage and $0.10 exit charge. Stops use a current eligible exact-mint quote, never an entry fallback, a stale quote, zero, or a historical invented execution. Missing prices leave the holding unresolved. Price gaps, polling, and provider delays may produce losses above 20%; enabling protection does not retroactively cap older losses.

Existing positions are priced and checked before source-wallet polling; protection also runs while entries are paused or the wallet-history source is unavailable. Stop closes are recorded with reason `stop_loss`, actual observation time, observed exit price, and the configured threshold. Pre-existing multiple lots still stop independently; source sells still close all copied lots for that source wallet and coin. The locked Real Copy Trade workspace inherits the canonical exposure/cooldown rules without enabling real execution.

Full 1,000-signature public-RPC pages are paged backwards before decoding and advancing the frozen cursor, so a busy wallet does not silently drop older signatures in the same observation window. This protects future coverage; it does not reconstruct gaps already skipped by older releases. Source provider errors and unsupported transactions can still limit coverage.

The older sections below describe prior releases where their entry-policy wording differs.

`/meme-coins/copy` is a separate virtual account with $100,000 capital and $50 per copied buy by default. It has its own state, permanent signal/close archives, cash, positions, start/stop and settings. It never connects a wallet, signs a transaction, or submits a swap. The existing scanner, its positions, learning batches and settings remain independent.

## Prospective selection and copying

Continuously page through Birdeye realized-profit gainers across 1W, 30d and 90d windows, 100 candidates per page up to the documented 10,000 offset range, then rotate windows. This replaces the old incorrectly parameterized wallet-value leaderboard. Rankings require at least $1,000 realized profit under the provider contract. Qualify two pending candidates per cycle with full-history positive realized profit, win rate above 50% and at least 20 sells. Enroll every qualifying discovered wallet; there is no five-trader cap. Preserve each enrollment timestamp, ranking window, qualification metrics and frozen swap provider. Failed qualification checks remain queued for retry. Existing checked wallets can be marked inactive for entries; owned lots retain sell monitoring. This is provider-limited historical discovery, not a claim to identify every trader or predict future winners.

Poll up to three wallets per cycle in oldest-polled order, independently of lexical wallet order, and check up to two fresh meme metadata records per cycle. This reserves space within the existing 45 Birdeye calls/minute budget. Large populations take longer to revisit and signals over 90 seconds old are explicitly skipped; the UI shows a nominal sweep estimate. There is no claim to capture every buy or sell.

Enroll each wallet at detection time, never backfill old winners. Poll frozen time windows with pagination and a two-second overlap. Buffer incomplete pages before processing them in time order. Deduplicate permanently by wallet, transaction, instruction, inner instruction, mint and side. Resume without copying old paused buys. Each new eligible BUY becomes a separate $50 lot (configurable). A source SELL, even partial, queues a full close of all that wallet's copied lots for the token. Different source wallets remain attributable separately. Owner manual exits carry exact lot identities and queue until a fresh original-pool observation exists; Sell all also pauses entries.

Only provider-identified meme tokens are copied. SOL, USDC and USDT are excluded. Category lookup is solely an asset-type check: **no liquidity, volume, momentum, second-price confirmation, grade, stop, target or scanner gate** controls copy entries. Missing/unsupported data, exhausted request capacity, stale signals over 90 seconds and inadequate virtual cash are explicitly skipped. At most two new metadata queries are attempted per cycle; more signals show a capacity reason. There is no claim that every wallet action can be captured.

Use fresh positive DEX Screener pool observations after wallet and category lookup; do not use source-trader fills or historical prices. Quotes must have been received within 45 seconds; current marks expire after 120 seconds. Held lots require their original pool; missing exits remain pending. Simulate the same costs as the scanner: 1% and $0.10 per side. These are observations and cost assumptions, not executable quotes or evidence of sellability. Latency and transaction attribution are saved.

A lease and revision CAS protect each cycle. Settings or manual changes invalidate a concurrent simulated cycle, including its event/close writes. Archives commit with cash and positions atomically. Retries cannot duplicate credits. No copy outcomes are fed into the scanner learner.

## Pool valuation recovery

A copied token may leave its original Pump.fun pool and trade on another pool. If the current listing omits the held pool, or explicitly reports it with no liquidity and zero five-minute volume, marks and queued PAPER exits may use a currently observed alternate with the **exact same base mint**, a recognized cash quote, positive reported liquidity, and positive five-minute volume and trade count. The highest reported liquidity among eligible alternates wins. Symbol matching, a bare alternate price, stale marks and entry prices cannot establish recovery. The original entry pool is retained as `entry_pool`, with timestamped `pool_changes`; quantity, entry cost, modeled costs and closed history are preserved. These are indicative provider prices and modeled proceeds, not guaranteed executable fills or market-impact estimates. This recovery criterion does not add an entry liquidity gate.

If any holding remains unpriced, total equity/P&L and comparison points remain null. The cards explicitly display a cash-plus-priced-holdings **subtotal** and **realized closed-trade P/L**, with the unpriced count and pending-total explanation. Missing assets are never silently valued at zero or represented as complete equity.


## Earnings-based completed-trade percentage

The Completed trades card shows realized net earnings divided by the sum of entry costs for all completed copied lots, multiplied by 100. Costs include the modeled entry charge; realized earnings already reflect modeled exit costs. This is a turnover-weighted trade return, not account return or a win percentage. Winning/total completed counts and average net earnings per completed lot are separately labeled. Open positions are excluded from both numerator and denominator. The backend aggregates all close records, independent of the 100-row UI history limit. No completed trades or incomplete historical entry costs leave the return unavailable rather than inventing a denominator.


## Separate crypto inactivity emails

An independent background worker checks both PAPER crypto accounts every minute. Each enabled bot receives its own no-trade email after 30 minutes without a successful recorded paper buy or sell. Skipped source signals, price refreshes and queued/unfilled exits do not count. The automatic bot uses committed opening/closing records; the copy bot uses successful buy events and persisted closes across its full history. Existing trading history seeds the initial timer; persisted monitoring state survives deployments. Observed pauses suppress and cancel pending alerts; resuming starts a fresh window. A trade re-arms that bot without affecting the other. There is one email per quiet episode, not repeated emails every scan.

The monitor reads both committed states under a database transaction and runs independently of the trading workers. It never changes trading settings or places orders. Messages distinguish copy/automatic bot, last trade/cycle, reported health/error and open holding count; quiet time alone is not proof of a fault. Existing RESEND_API_KEY and HEALTH_ALERT_EMAIL_FROM/TO deliver these explicitly requested alerts independently of the general health-email toggle. No credentials or raw provider bodies enter messages/errors.

A durable crypto_idle_mail outbox uses frozen payloads, stable provider idempotency keys, token-fenced leases and bounded retries. New activity/pauses cancel unsent pending emails.
