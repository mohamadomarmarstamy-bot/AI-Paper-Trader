# Persistent autonomous improvement backlog

Jarvis records paper operational bottlenecks as durable tasks: exit delays, heartbeat stalls, engine errors, chart gaps and low indicator coverage. Each task contains a fixed measurable goal, priority, original baseline, latest observed evidence, status and recurrence count. Queue/heartbeat/error work takes precedence over data-quality work and strategy tuning. Confirmed incident selection uses task priorities while retaining confirmation delays, three-investigation bounds, budget reservations and the existing allowed action/code scope. A task is never authority to change limits or deploy arbitrary code.

Cloud investigations receive the backlog and the matching selected goal. Applied investigation plans move the task to measuring; only sustained current observations over at least two minutes mark observed recovery. Owner pauses, absent/stale heartbeats and gaps in monitoring do not establish success. Recurring symptoms reopen recovered goals and increment their recurrence count. Persistent low coverage now triggers the existing ten-minute chart incident confirmation even when some indicators are available: below 20% complete across at least ten eligible candidates. Independent chart-repair checks require an increased complete count and at least 20% coverage when ten or more candidates remain. This threshold governs diagnostic recovery, not strategy promotion or trade admission.

Retained deployed repairs get independent metric follow-ups at most hourly. A recurrence is recorded without claiming the old repair caused it, especially after another release. An intentional owner pause is unverified. Follow-ups never revert an older release or force a deployment; persistent current symptoms go through the normal incident, test and rollback pipeline. Immediate candidate testing/verification/rollback remains in the existing isolated runner. Backlog and follow-up outcomes appear on System investigations.

No laptop-worker changes, new provider calls, budget increases, real-money permissions or accounting changes are included. This adds task management to existing bounded research/repair capabilities; it does not grant unrestricted self-rewriting or implement hypothetical skipped-coin outcomes.
