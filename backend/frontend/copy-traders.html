Trading settings

Changes affect this paper copy account. Lowering the lot cap leaves existing lots intact. Cooldown changes affect future exits; existing ladders keep their next target. Disabling trims keeps armed break-even protection.

Rules & limitations

Only fresh, supported source swaps are eligible. Signals older than 90 seconds, already-sold buys, duplicate adds at the coin limit, and buys during cooldown are skipped. Other programs may be unsupported. A source signal is not a paper fill.

Half-sales retain half the remaining quantity at each profit level. After the first trim, the remainder has a net break-even trigger. Source sells close its remaining copied quantity. Stops and exits need current prices; gaps and missing data can exceed the intended loss limit.

Modeled costs: 1% plus $0.10 per side, including each partial sale. Public RPC has limited coverage and may lag or rate-limit. Phantom shares a public address; this page never signs or submits real trades.

Discovery uses externally reported profit, win rate, activity and hold time. Wallet results are not independently audited and do not predict your copy results. Open Wallet details for each source report.
