How copying works

Continuously search paginated 7-day, 30-day and 90-day realized-profit rankings, and enroll every trader that passes the historical checks. Require positive full-history realized profit, a historical win rate above 50%, and at least 20 sells. Provider records and labels are not independently audited; rankings have a provider minimum of $1,000 realized profit. Wallet value is not used to rank profitability. New traders are followed only after enrollment.

Only new swaps are copied. SOL, USDC and USDT are excluded. Advanced providers require their meme category. Key-free mode accepts supported Pump/PumpSwap platform tokens and does not independently establish meme status; other programs are skipped. The provider’s category is not an independent safety check. A source sell, including a partial sell, closes all our copied lots for that wallet and coin. Pausing stops new copied buys; source sells and queued manual exits remain monitored.

Our entry is a fresh DEX Screener observation after detection, not the source trader’s earlier fill. Signals over 90 seconds old are skipped. No price means no invented buy or exit. Modeled costs match the scanner: 1% plus $0.10 per side. Observations do not verify an executable swap, sellability, slippage or liquidity. An optional Phantom connection shares only your public address. No signing keys, transaction submission, real swaps or real funds are used.
