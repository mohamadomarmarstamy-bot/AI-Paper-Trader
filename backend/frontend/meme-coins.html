How this version decides

Selection: up to eight Solana mints from your watchlist and latest token profiles; at least $100k reported pool liquidity, $20k five-minute volume, 40 recent transactions, and a 1.2 buy/sell activity ratio. Pair age: 30 minutes–30 days.

Entry: five-minute change between +2% and +20%, followed by +0.5% to +8% continuation across two observations of the same pool. Then check revoked mint/freeze authorities, supported legacy SPL program, and token-account concentration. No paid-boost ranking or ticker-only buys.

Exit: stop loss, take profit, trailing protection after activation, a 50% reported-liquidity drop, or maximum holding time. Each position keeps the rules recorded at entry. Re-entry cooldown: one hour. Missing prices leave exits unresolved.

Costs: simulations apply 1% per side plus $0.10 per side as a network-cost assumption. These are not actual quotes or fees. Prices have observation times; source market timestamps and sellability are unverified.

Paper settings

Daily loss brake uses marked virtual equity since UTC midnight. It blocks new buys; it does not guarantee exits or cap losses.
