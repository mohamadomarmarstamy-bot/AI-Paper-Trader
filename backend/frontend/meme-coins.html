Trading preferences

Exploration skips all entry filters and token checks. Trade size, position limit, daily loss brake, cooldown and exits remain active. Learning evaluates separate 50-close batches and reviews every 10 changes. It keeps settings unchanged when evidence is insufficient.

Off skips the wait and extra price-rise check. Liquidity, volume, momentum, token checks, and account limits still apply.

Liquidity and volume: USD minimums for new buys. Zero disables that minimum; missing data still skips the token.

Trading rules

Entries need your liquidity and volume minimums, recent buy activity, momentum, your optional second-price confirmation, and on-chain checks.

Exits follow the stop, target, trailing protection, holding time, or a 50% liquidity drop. Pausing stops new buys; exits stay active. Open positions keep their entry rules.

Prices are observations, not executable quotes. Costs model 1% + $0.10 per side. The daily loss brake uses marked equity since UTC midnight.
