Stock momentum entry filters

Broad automatic PAPER stock buys only. A zero numeric threshold disables that individual filter. Enabled filters reject missing evidence. The daily volume ratio applies only with intraday mode off; intraday mode uses the separate same-time threshold below. Float is provider-reported, cached up to 24 hours, and can be unavailable. Existing positions and manual Pro Ticker trades keep their separate controls. Existing score, freshness, and broker-risk gates still apply, including the legacy 8% ATR ceiling.

The learner compares entry conditions for winners and losers. With prospective validation enabled, a filter proposal must pass a separate future comparison before it changes saved settings. If a losing batch has no validated filter improvement, it reduces future position size by 10%, down to 0.1% of equity. It never raises exposure or loss limits. Small samples do not establish profitability.

Entry timing, execution estimates & rollback

Intraday checks use Alpaca IEX regular-session bars. Same-time volume replaces the daily volume threshold while this mode is enabled. It needs at least five comparable prior sessions and four completed bars today; missing or stale required evidence blocks a new buy. IEX covers one venue. VWAP and setups describe that feed, not the whole market.

100 basis points = 1%. Estimated costs are an extra stress allowance beyond broker fills; displayed broker P/L stays unchanged. Defaults subtract 10 basis points of slippage per side and zero additional fees. Costs and exit comparisons are estimates.

Only one filter experiment runs at a time. After 25 new fully closed trades entered under that exact settings revision, a follow-up mean return worse by over 0.1 percentage point triggers an audited rollback. A newer saved settings change supersedes the experiment. Defensive position-size cuts are never automatically reversed.
